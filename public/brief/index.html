FEDERAL CREDENTIAL September 2026

Pre-qualified as a Government of Canada AI supplier under all three bands.

Predictiv AI has been added to the Government of Canada's List of Interested AI Suppliers under all three supplier bands. Federal departments can now engage the Company directly on eligible work without a full open competitive procurement. The qualification spans the full platform: SHIFT AI (fleet), CloudRep (voice), and CloudMD (clinical).
